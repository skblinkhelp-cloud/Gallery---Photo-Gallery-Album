Privacy-First Guarantee: Gallery is built to work 100% offline. All photo browsing, automatic category organization, and 1-tap auto-enhancement execute locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or inspect your personal media.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Gallery - Photos Gallery & Album mobile application developed for Android devices.

2. 100% On-Device & Offline Architecture

  • Local Processing: All photo and video rendering, categorization, and editing happen on your device using hardware acceleration.
  • Smart Auto-Organization: Grouping photos into categories (People, Selfies, Nature, Animals, Documents, Videos, Movies) is performed entirely on-device with zero server-side transmission.
  • 1-Tap Auto-Enhance: All color matrix transformations, exposure adjustments, and filters run locally on your device GPU/CPU.

3. Media & Storage Access

  • Android Photo Picker: Gallery uses standard zero-permission Android Photo Picker and MediaStore APIs for media access. The app does not transmit broad storage data externally.
  • Folders & SD Card Storage: File transfer operations between internal memory and external SD cards are strictly executed on the local file system at your explicit direction.

4. Cloud Sync & Push Notifications

  • Optional Cloud Backup: If cloud sync is utilized, media transfers travel directly over encrypted HTTPS connections to your designated cloud storage. We do not store or inspect copies on third-party servers.
  • Push Notifications: The optional POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission is used solely to notify you when backup routines complete or when nightly on-device auto-organization finishes.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Data Protection

  • We do not partner with third-party data brokers.
  • We never sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal media with any third parties.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your content. Any photo, album, or folder deleted within Gallery is deleted immediately from your storage.
