# Privacy Policy for Gallery - Photos Gallery & Album

**Effective Date:** October 12, 2026  
**Developer Contact:** sk.blink.help@gmail.com  
**Application:** Gallery - Photos Gallery & Album (com.aistudio.gallery.pxkq)

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Gallery - Photos Gallery & Album**, a smart, light, and fast photo and video gallery. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your photos, videos, and personal information.

---

## 2. On-Device & Offline Architecture
- **100% On-Device Processing:** Gallery is built to work offline. All photo and video browsing, rendering, folder organization, and SD card file management take place entirely on your device.
- **On-Device Smart Organization:** Automatic grouping into categories (People, Selfies, Nature, Animals, Documents, Videos, and Movies) is computed locally using lightweight on-device classification algorithms. Your photos and facial/visual features are never uploaded to remote servers or third-party AI models for analysis.
- **1-Tap Auto-Enhance & Editing:** All image filters, color matrix adjustments, rotations, and tone adjustments are processed locally using your device’s GPU and CPU.

---

## 3. Media & Storage Access
- **Zero-Permission Photo Picker:** Gallery uses the official Android Photo Picker API (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia). This allows you to import photos and videos without granting broad storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, or READ_MEDIA_VIDEO).
- **Internal Storage & SD Card:** Folder management features allow you to view, move, and copy files between internal storage and SD cards. These file operations are strictly confined to the directories and files you explicitly manage within the application.

---

## 4. Cloud Sync & Push Notifications
- **Cloud Backup:** If you choose to enable cloud sync, backup requests are transmitted securely over HTTPS directly to your chosen cloud account. We never intercept, inspect, or retain copies of your uploaded photos.
- **Push Notifications:** The application requests POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission strictly to alert you when background sync completes, when nightly auto-organization runs, or when new media arrivals are detected. You can revoke notification permissions at any time via your device's Android System Settings.

---

## 5. Third-Party Data Sharing & Advertising
- **No Third-Party Tracking:** Gallery does not contain third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or data brokers.
- **No Data Selling:** We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal photos, videos, or metadata to any third parties.

---

## 6. Data Retention & User Control
- **Deletion Rights:** When you delete a photo, video, or folder inside Gallery, it is removed immediately from your device storage.
- **Data Portability:** You can export, copy, or move your photos to an SD card or external drive at any time.
